635 Central · power at a glance

read locally from the device or meter reported by a vendor cloud estimated from other meters not metered — a remainder

House load is solar plus grid, so it is only shown when all three are fresh. A dash means no current reading, never zero. The large number is now; the small one after the slash is the average over the previous 24 hours, in the same unit, taken over the time actually seen. A ~ means less than 90% of the day was seen (solar is only recorded by day, so its average is a daytime one); a dash means less than 40%. Gas arrives as whole days, a day or two late, so its number is the latest posted day's average (1 therm = 29.3 kWh of heat, so about 1.2 kW for a therm a day) against the day before; it is heat, not electricity, and isn't part of the house load. The graph icons open the matching Grafana dashboard. Moving dots show current flowing; the PG&E wire reverses when the house is exporting.
